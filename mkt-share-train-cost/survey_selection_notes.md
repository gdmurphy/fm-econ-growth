# Survey Source Selection Notes

---

## Menlo Ventures Survey (Nov 2024–Jan 2025)

495 U.S. enterprise AI decision makers surveyed Nov 7, 2024–Jan 25, 2025. Market shares based on self-reported production API usage, weighted by firm scale, triangulated with public financials. U.S.-only, enterprise procurement lens, filters to production usage only.

---

## OpenRouter 2025 Revenues

Aggregate revenue through the OpenRouter routing platform. User base is developer/builder-heavy ([app and agent leaderboard](https://openrouter.ai/apps)), with a large programming community and diverse global presence ([arXiv:2601.10088](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10088v1#S5), [SimilarWeb traffic data](https://www.similarweb.com/website/openrouter.ai/#demographics)). Only sees traffic routed through the platform; direct enterprise API agreements are excluded.

---

## Ramp Data

Business spend data from Ramp's U.S. corporate card platform. 2,200+ customers with $100k+ annualized revenue. AI Index provides coarse sector-level adoption; business size data can be approximated via [TheirStack job-listing analysis](https://theirstack.com/en/technology/ramp-com), though that method has its own selection on companies with active tech hiring.

---

## a16z CIO Survey (Jan 2026)

100 VPs and C-level executives in Global 2000 companies. All companies had $500M+ annual revenue (88% over $1B, 30% over $10B); 50%+ had 10k+ employees, 80% were multinational. Industries: financial services (15%), software and technology (12%), manufacturing (11%), healthcare (10%), retail (10%), professional services (8%).

## CBA API Spend (Jun–Nov 2025)

Actual API spending by CBA customers June–November 2025. Customer base has been segmented by industry; business size measures are under development.

## Revenue Estimates (2025)

Estimates derived from financial disclosures, analyst reports, and public statements. Most frontier AI labs are private, so figures carry wide confidence intervals. Total revenue blends consumer subscriptions, enterprise contracts, and API usage; Google, Meta, xAI, and DeepSeek are not comparably covered.
